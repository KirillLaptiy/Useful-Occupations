Tired of the boring vanilla professions? Want to truly master the apocalypse and become an unstoppable force? Useful Occupations is a massive expansion that adds a ton of new content, making your game more complex, immersive, and incredibly fun!

This mod features:
I. New Professions: Scout, Veteran, and Special Purpose Master. Each comes with unique starter kits, custom skill sets, and distinct gameplay styles.
II. Over 20 Unique Traits: Become Invincible, gain an Iron Mind, master the Marksman path, or fight as a Trained Fighter. Want to move like a ghost? Use the Inconspicuous trait to vanish from the undead!
III. Deep Crafting System: Forget basic scavenging. Forge carbon weapons and armor, master mold-casting (resin, clay, cement), smelt metal, and assemble complex mechanical parts!
IV. MRE (Meal, Ready-to-Eat) System: Craft military ration bags ranging from 5kg to 50kg! Pack food, meds, and fluids, perfectly preserving all their stats for your long expeditions
V. Lockpicking & Stealth Mechanics: Pick locks, disable alarms, sneak silently, and enjoy walking like a zombie!

IMPORTANT: TWO VERSIONS AVAILABLE!
I. Full Version: Includes Professions, Traits, Items, Crafting, and all Mechanics. Perfect for a complete overhaul
II. Traits Only Version: A lightweight version containing only the new Traits and Professions. Perfect for players who want the new character builds without changing the vanilla item and crafting balance

Survival will never be the same. Who will you become?
